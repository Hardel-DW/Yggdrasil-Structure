## Yggdrasil - Minecraft 26.2
Yggdrasil is now compatible with Minecraft 26.2.

### Update
- Pack format updated to 107 (26.2).
- The Runic Fracture door advancement has been migrated to the new namespaced entity predicate format introduced in 26.2 (`minecraft:entity_type`, `minecraft:entity_tags`), replacing the old `type` / `nbt` fields.

No gameplay change in this release, it is a compatibility update. Have fun, and as usual the mod version is available for those who prefer it.
